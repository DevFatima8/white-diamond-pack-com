## Brand & Style

The design system projects a high-grade industrial polish tailored for both commercial enterprise buyers (B2B wholesale, food service chains, hotel procurement) and retail consumers (B2C direct ordering). It fuses hygienic pristine minimalism with authoritative corporate weight, evoking uncompromising hygiene, manufacturing scale, and logistical dependability.

The visual style combines **Corporate Modern** with **High-Contrast Precision**. Clean architectural white fields prevent packaging products from feeling cluttered or commodity-grade, while deep saturated navy anchors the technical credibility. Dynamic accents of vibrant red demand direct conversion and transactional focus, balanced by indigo-tinted structure and micro-details such as vertical running track labels, crisp metadata grids, and balanced radii.

## Colors

The palette establishes strict functional clarity:

- **Primary Action (`#E63946`)**: High-urgency red reserved exclusively for conversion mechanics, active CTAs, shopping carts, bulk quote requests, status badges, and primary pricing. On hover, transitions cleanly to `#C92A37`.
- **Secondary Accent (`#6366F1`)**: Modern indigo-purple functioning as structural accenting, active filter states, tab indicators, and categorical tags.
- **Surface Tint (`#EEF2FF`)**: An airy, ultra-light cool purple-blue tint utilized for alternate background striping, subtle callout cards, and active navigation pills.
- **Tertiary Accent / Deep Sinks (`#1E1B4B` & `#12102E`)**: Monolithic purple-navy tones providing visual gravity across persistent top bars, executive enterprise megamenus, footers, and industrial technical specs.
- **Neutrals**: Pure `#FFFFFF` serves as the canvas for pristine hygiene. Copy is driven by `#0F172A` (heading/body contrast) and `#64748B` (secondary data/specs). Borders are fixed to `#E2E8F0`.

### Color Rules
- **No Green or Orange:** Statuses typically requiring green or orange must utilize monochromatic neutrals or distinct primary/secondary badges with explicit iconographic signifiers.
- **Gradient Restriction:** Gradients are strictly limited to linear dual-stops running from `#E63946` to `#6366F1` at an angle of 135 degrees. Used strictly for hero badges, key metric highlights, and selective marketing ribbons.

## Typography

Typography establishes clear demarcation between structural manufacturing clarity and human accessibility:

- **Headlines (`Outfit`)**: Geometric, wide, and crisp. Outfit delivers structural, modern industrial confidence suitable for manufacturing scale and equipment precision.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, highly legible, and open. Neutral enough to avoid visual clutter across dense SKU sheets, packaging dimensions, and certifications.
- **Vertical Sub-Labels (`label-vertical`)**: Rendered in all-caps Outfit with heavy letter-spacing (`0.14em`) rotated -90 degrees on left section margins to serve as categorical index markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with an upper layout constraint of 1440px to retain executive focus on wide desktop displays:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Alternate B2B catalog views unlock a 280px fixed-width left rail for SKU filters, specs, and material categorizations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 32px margins. Filter rails collapse into sliding side sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Product listings snap to 2-column or single-card stack.

### Spatial Rhythm
Sections alternate rhythmically between clean `#FFFFFF` ground and structural `#EEF2FF` backing bands. Content modules within packaging catalog displays must employ `space-lg` to `space-xl` block separations to mirror pharmaceutical and food-grade hygiene standards.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined using ultra-soft, corporate drop shadows with low opacity and slight purple-slate diffusion, avoiding dark muddy tones:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Pure white background with `#E2E8F0` crisp 1px borders. Baseline state for data tables, dimension charts, and technical specifications.
- **Level 1 (Card Default)**: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Used for base product cards and packaging catalog modules on white or tinted grounds.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Card)**: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04)`. Applied to active hover states on interactive cards, lifting the element and subtly illuminating the card outline.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts / Modals / Cart Drawers)**: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(18, 16, 46, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(18, 16, 46, 0.08)`. Reserved for persistent search bars, quick-quote dialogs, and navigation flyouts.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs controlled, modern curvature:

- **Standard Base Radii (12px / `0.75rem`)**: Applied to all core cards, inputs, secondary badges, and catalog panels.
- **Elevated Interactive Radii (16px / `1rem`)**: Applied to hero panels, floating quotation drawers, and promotional packaging modules.
- **Pill Formats (`rounded-full`)**: Strictly reserved for primary conversion buttons, pricing chips, and vertical category tags.
- **Table & Spec Radii**: Spec data tables maintain crisp outer corners of 12px with square 0px internal cell intersections.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Purchase & Direct Quote)**: Solid `#E63946` background, `#FFFFFF` text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), font weight 600. Hover transitions seamlessly to `#C92A37`. Focus rings use `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(230, 57, 70, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary (Wholesale Spec / Sample Request)**: Outlined `#E2E8F0` border, `#1E1B4B` text, `#FFFFFF` background. Hover shifts to `#EEF2FF` background and `#6366F1` border.
- **Tertiary / Corporate Action**: Solid `#1E1B4B` deep navy, `#FFFFFF` text, rounded 12px. Used in corporate portals and B2B vendor logins.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Category Filter Pills**: `#EEF2FF` background with `#6366F1` text. On selection, shifts to solid `#6366F1` with white text.
- **Stock / Food-Grade Compliance Badges**: Neutral slate `#0F172A` background with crisp white uppercase text (`label-md`), conveying industrial certification (e.g., "FDA APPROVED", "PFT-FREE").

### Product & Spec Cards
- Base surface in `#FFFFFF`, with 1px border `#E2E8F0` and `rounded-lg` (12px–16px).
- Upper packaging showcase zone on ultra-subtle off-white background (`#F8FAFC`) with aspect ratio 4:3.
- Spec metadata rendered in tabular key-value rows using `body-sm` (`#64748B` keys, `#0F172A` bold values).
- Hover applies Level 2 elevation and translates the card `-2px` vertically.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 1px border `#E2E8F0` on white, internal padding `12px 16px`, corner radius 12px.
- Focus state activates a 1px border in `#6366F1` alongside a 3px ambient ring of `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px x 20px boxes with 6px corner radius for checkboxes, full circular for radios.
- Unchecked: `#E2E8F0` border with white surface.
- Checked: Solid `#6366F1` fill displaying a sharp white stroke checkmark.

### Vertical Section Indicators
- Left-aligned vertical track element positioned alongside major product sections.
- Combines a continuous 1px `#E2E8F0` rail with an Outfit `label-vertical` indicator tracking the manufacturing category (e.g., "DISPOSABLE CUTLERY & VENDING") in uppercase indigo.
